Six Can Chicken Tortilla Soup

Overview

  • Prep Time:
    5 mins
  • Cook Time:
    15 mins
  • Total Time:
    20 mins
  • Servings:
    6

Why you'll love this recipe

  • This soup relies on cannned ingredients for a fuss-free pantry dinner.

  • Many reviewers recommend adding your favorite spices and seasonings to jazz up the flavor.

  • All recipes member Rolanda writes, "So quick, easy, and cheap ... just open the cans and boil."

Ingredients

  • 2 (14.5 ounce) cans chicken broth
  • 1 (15 ounce) can whole kernel corn, drained
  • 1 (15 ounce) can black beans
  • 1 (10 ounce) can chunk chicken
  • 1 (10 ounce) can diced tomatoes with green chile peppers, drained
  • crushed tortilla chips, for serving (Optional)
  • shredded Cheddar cheese, for serving (Optional)

Steps on how to cook are as follows accordingly

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Pour chicken broth, corn. black beans, chunk chicken, and diced tomatoes with green chiles into a large saucepan or stockpot over medium heat.
  3. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until chicken is heated through, about 15 minutes.
  4. Serve hot, topped with tortilla chips and shredded cheese.
