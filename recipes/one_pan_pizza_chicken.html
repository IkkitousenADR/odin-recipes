Pizza Chicken: One-Pan Weeknight Dinner Casserole Craze

Craving pizza without the crust? Meet One-Pan Pizza Chicken. Thinly pounded chicken breasts loaded with marinara, melty cheese, pepperoni, and fresh veggies—all baked in one pan in under 20 minutes.

Chef's Tips

  • Consider the type of chicken:

    We call for boneless skinless chicken breast here, but feel free to substitute with boneless skinless chicken thighs. You could even use chicken tenders!

  • Pound it out:

    The easiest way to pound chicken is to place it in a resealable plastic bag, remove any air, seal the bag, and use the flat side of a meat mallet or a rolling pin to pound. If you’re not up for that task, consider buying cutlets instead to save time.

  • Choosing sauce:

    If you have a pizza sauce you love, feel free to swap it in for the crushed tomatoes and seasonings used here. And use as much or as little as you like.

  • Customize seasonings:

    Speaking of the seasonings, add or subtract what you like from the recipe. For example, substitute freshly minced garlic for the garlic powder or fresh oregano for the Italian seasoning.

  • Use your favorite cheese:

    We tried mozzarella but found that we wanted more flavor. Swapping it with Italian blend style shredded cheese added just the right touch. If you can’t find an Italian blend, mix some Parmesan or Fontina cheese with shredded mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • 1 (15-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, divided
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder, divided
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder, divided
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 1/2 pounds chicken breast, pounded to 1/4-inch thickness
  • 1/8 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 3/4 cup shredded Italian blend cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced pepperoni
  • 1 (2.25 ounce) can sliced black olives
  • 1/4 cup diced green bell pepper

Steps on how to cook are as follows accordingly

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C)

  2. Spread 1/2 of the crushed tomatoes over the button of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Add 1/4 teaspoon salt, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/2 teaspoon onion powder, and 1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning, and stir to combine.

  3. Lay chicken on top of the tomatoes and season with the remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt and pepper.

  4. Cover chicken with remaining crushed tomatoes the drizzle with vinegar. Sprinkle remaining 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/2 teaspoon onion powder, and 1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning over everything, then cover with cheese.

  5. Top with pepperoni, black olives, and peppers.

  6. Bake until cheese is bubblign and melted and chicken has reached an internal temperature of at least 165 degrees F (74 degrees C), about 20 minutes.
